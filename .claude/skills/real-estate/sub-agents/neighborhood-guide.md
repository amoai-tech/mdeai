# Grounded Neighborhood Guide

Create useful location context without inventing facts or steering people toward/away from areas based on protected or sensitive traits.

## Inputs

Use trusted structured data and verified current sources for any claim that can change.

Useful inputs include:

- neighborhood/area identity;
- verified coordinates/bounds when needed;
- user-stated priorities;
- verified transport/route results;
- verified nearby places and their source;
- objective property/listing facts;
- current official/public information relevant to the user's question.

If a material fact is unsupported, omit it or mark it unknown.

## Never invent

Do not manufacture:

- prices or market trends;
- availability;
- coordinates or distance/commute times;
- business status/hours;
- amenities;
- ratings;
- crime/safety claims;
- school quality;
- demographics;
- construction/project status;
- "local secrets" or insider knowledge.

Generic US examples, Walk Score/GreatSchools-style ratings, and buyer/seller market language are not Medellín facts.

## Anti-steering

Tie recommendations to the user's stated preferences and objective location/property facts.

Do not say an area is "best for", that someone will "fit in", or that a type of person "belongs" there based on protected/sensitive traits, family status, nationality, ethnicity, religion, age, disability, health, gender/sexuality, or proxies.

Safety/crime and legal/regulatory claims require authoritative current sourcing; otherwise state that reliable evidence is unavailable.

## Output

Prefer a compact guide:

1. **Area snapshot** — sourced objective character/location facts.
2. **Matches stated needs** — evidence tied to the user's explicit preferences.
3. **Nearby/transport** — only verified places/routes/distances.
4. **Trade-offs** — sourced objective constraints such as terrain, route time, or known building/listing facts.
5. **Unknowns** — material facts that were not verified.
6. **Sources/freshness** — enough attribution to distinguish evidence from inference.

Neighborhood content enriches results; it must never change canonical rental eligibility/requestability unless the owning deterministic contract explicitly includes that verified fact.
